HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
======================

Drop files into these folders. The website finds them by itself:
no code to change.

steps/    Photos of the build, named with the step number first.
          01-the-box.jpg, 01-all-parts.jpg   -> step 1 (Unboxing the kit)
          02-binding-the-radio.jpg           -> step 2 (Radio control)
          03-arms-on-the-body.jpg            -> step 3 ...
          The first photo of each step is shown large, the rest as small
          thumbnails. The words after the number become the caption:
          "03-arms-on-the-body.jpg" is shown as "Arms on the body".

flight/   Photos and short videos (.mp4) of the drone flying.
          They go into the gallery in name order.

team/     Portraits: 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg
          (same order as the team members in src/content.js).
          They are shown in black and white to match the design.

logs/     Screenshots of your log graphs from Mission Planner.

TIPS
----
- Use .jpg, .png or .webp. iPhone .heic photos do not work in most
  browsers: export them as JPG first (or set the iPhone camera to
  Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible).
- Phone photos are big. Resize them to about 2000 pixels wide so the site
  loads fast. Keep each video under 20 MB.
- Do not create folders inside these folders.

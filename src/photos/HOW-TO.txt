HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
======================

Drop files into these folders. The website finds them by itself:
no code to change. One folder per section, under src/photos/:

home/home_title/      Drone cut-out for the title (PNG, transparent).
home/home_team/       Portraits: 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg (same order as the team in src/content.js).
home/home_intro/, home/home_prwph1/   Other home sections.
phase-1/ph1_step01 ... ph1_step06/    Photos of each build step.
phase-1/ph1_flight/, ph1_hif_*/       Flight and "how it flies" photos and videos (.mp4).
parts_drone/          Cut-out images of the kit parts.

Files show in name order. The words after a leading number become the
caption: "03-arms-on-the-body.jpg" is shown as "Arms on the body".

TIPS
----
- Use .jpg, .png or .webp. iPhone .heic does not work in most browsers.
- Resize photos to about 2000 px wide. Keep each video under 20 MB.
